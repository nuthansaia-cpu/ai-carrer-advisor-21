🎯 What This Platform Does

AI Career Advisor is an explainable AI-powered career intelligence platform that bridges the gap between a user's current skills and the requirements of the modern job market. By combining skill extraction, career matching, skill-gap analysis, and personalized learning roadmaps, it helps students and job seekers make informed career decisions and become better prepared for meaningful employment.

❓ The Four Questions We Answer
🔹 What am I good at?
We identify and map your technical and soft skills from your profile or resume.
🔹 Which careers match my profile?
Our AI compares your skills against 10 career role profiles and ranks them by fit score.
🔹 What skills am I missing?
Skill-gap analysis highlights exactly which skills you need, with 🔴 High / 🟡 Medium / 🟢 Low priority.
🔹 What should I learn next?
A personalized month-by-month roadmap guides you from your current level to job-readiness.
🤖 AI Architecture
Layer 1 — Skill Extraction: Regex + 150+ alias dictionary normalizes raw text to canonical skill names.
Layer 2 — Career Matching: Weighted scoring (70% required skills, 30% preferred) with bonuses for education, experience, projects, certifications, and interests.
Layer 3 — Gap Analysis: Every skill tagged by priority — High (required, missing), Medium (preferred), Low (optional).
Layer 4 — Explainability: Each recommendation includes a human-readable reason explaining the match.
Layer 5 — Roadmap Generation: Month-by-month learning plans derived from the career knowledge base.
⚖ Responsible AI Principles
✅ Transparency — every recommendation explains why it was made
✅ Fairness — no demographic attributes affect recommendations
✅ Explainability — skill match details shown, not a black-box score
✅ Privacy — only career-relevant information is collected
✅ Growth mindset — low scores framed as opportunities, never rejection
✅ Human agency — AI provides guidance; the user makes final decisions
